What makes it sitNL

For developers, architects and BI/data people who work with SAP technology, whether you've been part of the community for years or this is your first sitNL.

  • Two tracks

    A tech track, and the BI/data track.

  • The pub quiz

    Hosted by quizmaster Dick Groenhof. Bring a team, or join one on the spot.

  • Run by volunteers

    No ticket price and no sales pitches from the stage. Sponsors cover the costs, so the day stays free. This year's team: Twan van den Broek, Jan-Willem Kaagman, Ronald Konijnenburg, Tim Koster, Jan Penninkhof, Wim Snoep, Tamas Szirtes and Caspar van Tergouw.

Previous editions

Call for papers

    Open route

    By car

    Take exit 20 (Rosmalen) from the A2. Follow the Reitscheweg, turn onto the Amerikastraat, and the office is on your right.

    Parking

    The office has its own parking deck with plenty of space. Parking is free.

    Public transport

    From ’s-Hertogenbosch station, take bus 6 towards Empel and get off at Europalaan, the 9th stop. The office is a 2-minute walk.
