What makes it sitNL

  • Two tracks

    A tech track, and the BI/data track.

  • The pub quiz

    Hosted by quizmaster Dick Groenhof. Bring a team, or join one on the spot.

  • Run by volunteers

    No ticket price and no sales pitches from the stage. Sponsors cover the costs, so the day stays free.

Call for papers

Built something with SAP technology that others could learn from? Tell us about it. First-time speakers are welcome, and we'll help you shape the talk.

What we're looking for

Real projects over product overviews: what you built, what broke, and what you'd do differently next time. Tech and BI/data topics both fit.

Open route

By car

Take exit 20 (Rosmalen) from the A2. Follow the Reitscheweg, turn onto the Amerikastraat, and the office is on your right.

Parking

The office has its own parking deck with plenty of space. Parking is free.

Public transport

From ’s-Hertogenbosch station, take bus 6 towards Empel and get off at Europalaan, the 9th stop. The office is a 2-minute walk.

Registration opens soon

Registration is free and opens on this page; until then, put both dates in your calendar.
